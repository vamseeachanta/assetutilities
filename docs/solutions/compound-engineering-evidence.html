Preserving unknown results in plugin trials

Summary

A plugin trial can produce useful preparation without establishing plugin effectiveness. A working evidence validator, a clean worktree, and authored guidance do not establish that a plugin installed cleanly or improved a task. This distinction applies to the Compound Engineering trial.

Problem and reusable method

When execution restrictions prevent a live trial, mark the relevant criteria NOT_EVALUATED and state the missing observation. Do not mark them PASS because a preparatory artifact exists, or FAIL because execution was not attempted.

Pin the candidate plugin revision, retain observations, and record evidence digests before judging outcomes. Require the complete criterion set and re-read the evidence when producing a summary. A digest mismatch invalidates the record until the evidence is reconciled; it does not justify silently refreshing a hash.

Keep adoption separate from evidence completeness. A fully populated record can be ready for review while the maintainer still needs to reject or limit adoption. Confidence, usefulness, and reuse require a recorded judgment or measurement against a stated criterion.

Vocabulary

Observation
A recorded result from actually running the candidate workflow under identified conditions.
Evidence artifact
Retained bytes supporting an observation, addressed by a relative path and digest.
NOT_EVALUATED
A missing observation with a reason; distinct from a failed trial or a measured zero.
Readiness
Completeness and local byte agreement sufficient to request maintainer review; not approval or rollout authority.

Next use and verification

Start with the pending record and the worked example and contract. Replace a criterion only after collecting its observation and checking the supporting artifact. Synthetic CLI tests verify record handling, including tampering and escaping paths. This note is authored guidance, not plugin-produced compound output; time saved on a later occurrence has not been measured.
